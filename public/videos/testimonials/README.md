# Testimonial video delivery

The testimonial videos are served locally from this directory:

- `alan-waggoner-testimonial.mp4` (118 MB)
- `ashley-stanford-testimonial.mp4` (25.7 MB)

Their poster images are captured from video frames and live in
`public/images/testimonials/`. If video delivery moves to a reviewed CDN, R2, or
Stream URL later, update the shared content record in `src/lib/testimonials.ts`.
