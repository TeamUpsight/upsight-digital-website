# Technology marks

The local SVGs in this directory are sourced from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/),
whose artwork repository is published under CC0-1.0, with a small number of local
product-style utility marks for products without a Simple Icons source. Brand names
and marks remain their respective owners' trademarks. They are decorative product
identifiers beside visible names, not certification or partnership claims.

`power-bi.svg` is the unaltered 48px color Power BI mark from Microsoft's
`@fabric-msft/svg-icons` package. `snowflake.svg` is the blue bug with registration
mark from Snowflake's official logo package. `amazon-marketing-cloud.png` is the
unaltered asset supplied by the site owner.

`firebase.svg`, `mixpanel.svg`, and `posthog.svg` use the corresponding colored
brand paths from Simple Icons v16.32.0 (CC0-1.0), stored locally. `amplitude.svg`
and `branch.svg` are official colored logos from the [Amplitude brand guide](https://brand.amplitude.com/creative/logo)
and [Branch brand guide](https://www.branch.io/brand/), respectively. The local
`appsflyer.png`, `adjust.png`, and `kochava.png` assets are supplied by the site
owner. `firebase.svg` remains the clean local Firebase brand mark because the
supplied Firebase PNG has a checkerboard background baked into its pixels. All
names and marks remain their respective owners' trademarks. No partnership or
certification is implied.
