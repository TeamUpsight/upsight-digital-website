# Domain context

## Company and positioning

**Upsight Digital** is a technical digital analytics consultancy and analytics implementation partner. The current site represents support for marketing agencies, ecommerce businesses, website owners and brands that need reliable digital measurement. It serves clients remotely worldwide; its repository-backed organization data names Dubai, UAE.

Current services represented in source include GA4 and GTM implementation, server-side tracking/sGTM, Meta Conversions API, consent management/CMP and Consent Mode v2, ecommerce tracking, tracking audits, dashboards/reporting and analytics measurement architecture. App/MMP capability may be described only where the current approved source specifically supports it.

## Voice and vocabulary

Use technically credible, consultative, practical, clear and confident language. Prefer precise terms such as *measurement architecture*, *implementation*, *validation*, *attribution*, *data quality*, *consent-aware* and *server-side tracking*. Explain the operational benefit without promising a commercial outcome.

Avoid hype, guaranteed ROAS/revenue claims, fear-based privacy language and vague "best-in-class" phrasing. Distinguish improved analytics accuracy from a guaranteed business result. Do not imply legal advice or a compliance guarantee.

## Content guardrails

Client metrics must come from approved case-study material. Keep testimonials verbatim and approved unless authorization says otherwise. Never invent awards, certifications, partners, client names, customer relationships or capabilities. New public claims should be factual, dated where material, and easy for a human owner to verify.

## Evidence classes

### Verified in repository

Facts explicitly present in approved website copy, case-study source, organization/schema data or other current public repository material. Preserve their qualified wording and link the relevant page where useful.

### Human confirmation required

New customer counts; experience durations when dates change; certifications; partnerships; client names; case-study metrics; pricing; legal/compliance guarantees; and geographical or business-hour details.

### Prohibited to invent

Testimonials, customer metrics, client relationships, legal certifications, security certifications and awards.
